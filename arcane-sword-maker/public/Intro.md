# ⚔️ Arcane Sword Maker

> Craft the weapon with the longest, most legendary name in history!

Each time you enchant a weapon, you embue it with a powerful name.

---

With each enchant, there's a small chance of embuing it with an even more powerful name than usual. The longer the name, the better&nbsp;&mdash; and the more rare!

---

## How to Play

- Drag a weapon from the inbox to the work area
- Enchant the weapon, and behold the name!
- Drag completed weapons to the output area.

May your blades be sharp and well-named!
